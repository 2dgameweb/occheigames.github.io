PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Games Arcade ("we", "us", "the Site") collects, uses and protects information from users ("you", "the user") when you use the games and pages available on this website.

1. Data Controller

The data controller is:

Davide Bellomo
Contact email: davidebellomo1@icloud.com

2. Personal Data We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number or physical address). Our games require no registration, no login and no account.

The only data stored is technical game data, saved locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage, including:

  • Best scores and game records
  • In-game currencies (gems, crystals)
  • Unlocked items, skins and backgrounds
  • Preferences (sound, music, equipped items)
This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers, because we do not operate any servers that store user data.

3. Cookies & Advertising (Google AdSense)

This Site displays advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including advertising cookies) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party cookies at www.aboutads.info.

For details on how Google handles data, please read the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

  • Locally stored game data is used only to make the games work (saving progress, scores and purchases on your device).
  • Advertising cookies are used by Google to display ads that keep the Site free.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any personal data, because we do not collect any.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Game data stored in localStorage remains on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data / cache for this website, or by using your browser's "clear browsing data" option.

6. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, most of these rights are automatically respected. For any privacy question or request, contact us at davidebellomo1@icloud.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Our games are family friendly and do not knowingly collect any personal information from children (or anyone). Parents can let children play safely: no registration, no chat, no personal data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the new policy.

9. Contact

For any question about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: davidebellomo1@icloud.com
