ROCKET RISE
Climb as high as you can! Hold to ignite, avoid hazards, repair your ship.
🏅 Best: 0 m  •  Crystals: 0
HOW TO PLAY
Hold the screen or press SPACE to ignite the engine and climb fast.
The ship drifts sideways at a fixed speed: drag or use arrow keys / A D.
With the engine off you climb slowly, but must reignite within 10s or fall.
Hazards unlock as you climb: asteroids first, then flammable zones, maintenance, black holes and aliens.
Flammable zones: cross with engine OFF and don't move. Asteroids and enemy shots never reach them.
Alien ships fire a couple of shots and then fly away sideways.
Black holes are huge and pull you in when the engine is OFF. Keep it on to pass safely.
Maintenance zones (wrench sign): finish the repair in time - wipe, cables, leak or valve.
Crystals are saved forever; spend them in the shop for new rockets. Boosters give turbo + invincibility.
