ABOUT MINI GAMES ARCADE

Mini Games Arcade is an independent collection of free browser games. Our mission is simple: fun, bite-sized games you can play instantly — no downloads, no installs, no account required. Just open the site and press play, on your phone, tablet or computer.

Every game is hand-built, carefully tested and updated regularly. Your best scores, crystals and unlocked items are saved privately on your own device — we don't ask for personal data and we never will. New mini-games are added all the time, so keep an eye on the "Coming Soon" slot!

We are a small indie team making games with passion. If you have ideas, feedback or partnership requests, we would love to hear from you.
